How we work

Most of the value is decided before anything is built.

Three stages, deliberately uneven. A solution is only as good as the understanding behind it.

01

Discovery

Where most of the value is decided.

We learn how you work, then let AI absorb the busywork.

NeedWhat the business is trying to do, not just what was asked.
WorkflowWalked end to end, with the people who do it.
ObservationWe watch real work. Descriptions and reality often differ.
ToolsWhat you already use, who maintains it, and where data lives.
TimeWhere it is lost repeatedly, not occasionally.
SuccessMeasurable, and agreed before anything is built.

Discovery may find a workflow should not be automated. That is a valid outcome.

02

Build

ScopeSized to what discovery found.
SequenceOne workflow first, not the business.
ProofProven in real use before adding more.
03

Extend

CadenceOne at a time, each proven first.
SpeedEach pass is faster. The base exists.
SupportGrows as more workflows come in.

A working demo can come early when it helps you see the value. Never a substitute for discovery.

What the work can turn into.

Three levels. Not a ladder to climb. Which one fits is set by the workflow, not by ambition.

01

Connect

Predictable tasks with one right answer.

What it isOff-the-shelf tools wired into your apps. Listings, social, a simple site.
ExamplesA web inquiry creates a customer record. Invoices build from finished jobs.
LimitsCannot handle judgment calls or systems unique to your business.
02

Assist

Judgment needed, so a person stays in.

What it isA light assistant trained on your information. A person approves it all.
ExamplesQuote drafts from past pricing. Replies drafted from your own policies.
LimitsCannot act on its own.
03

Act

Work moves across tools without handoffs.

What it isA custom system that acts across your tools without step-by-step approval.
ExamplesAn inquiry books the slot and confirms it. A finished job sends the invoice.
LimitsMore setup and upkeep. Every unattended step is one you have to trust.

Plenty of workflows are fully served at Level 1.

If existing tools cover most of it, customize the edges.

What changes with company size.

Size does not set the level. Any size can land at any level.

Under 20 people

Nobody maintains software, so low upkeep beats capability.

20 to 200 people

Several teams share it, so documentation and access matter.

Above 200 people

Governance and data rules mean approval outlasts the build.

Keeping it right-sized.

These tools change quickly. The best solution today may not be the best one in six months. Part of ongoing support is telling you when something you pay for can be simplified, replaced by a standard feature, or removed. It cuts both ways. Sometimes the answer is less, not more.

Scope and cost come after discovery.

No figures until we both know what the work is.
